# Warranty

## What is covered
Every product has a two-year warranty against faults in materials and workmanship, starting on the date of delivery.

## Tents
Tent seams and zips are covered for five years. Tent poles are covered for two years, like other parts.

## What is not covered
Normal wear, damage from misuse, accidents, fire, and repairs made by anyone other than us. Waterproof coatings lose performance over time; this is normal wear and is not covered.

## How to make a claim
Email support with your order number and photos of the fault. We will repair, replace or refund the product. You do not need to send the product back until we ask for it.
