# Camping stove CS-400: user manual

## Before first use
Check the burner and the valve for damage. Use only gas canisters with a screw thread (EN 417), such as our gas canister GC-420.

## Connecting the canister
Close the valve fully. Screw the stove onto the canister by hand until it is firm. Do not use tools.

## Lighting the stove
Open the valve a quarter turn and press the ignition button. If the stove does not light after three presses, close the valve, wait one minute and try again.

## Safety
Never use the stove inside a tent, a car or any closed space: it produces carbon monoxide, which can kill. Keep the stove at least one metre away from the tent and anything flammable. Never leave a lit stove unattended.

## Troubleshooting
If the stove will not light, the canister may be empty or too cold: below 0°C the gas does not vaporise well. Warm the canister in your hands or a pocket. A yellow, smoky flame means the burner is dirty; clean it with a soft brush.

## Cleaning and storage
Let the stove cool before removing the canister. Store canisters below 50°C and away from direct sunlight.
