# Returns policy

## Return window
You can return most products within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days start on the day the parcel is delivered, not the day you ordered.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, clean and in their original packaging with all labels attached. Tents and sleeping bags must be returned folded in their own bag. We cannot accept items that show signs of outdoor use, such as mud, water stains or a smell of smoke.

## Items that cannot be returned
For safety and hygiene reasons, some products cannot be returned once opened: gas canisters, insect repellent and sunscreen. Unopened, they can be returned like any other product.

## How to return
Start a return from your order page. You will receive a prepaid label by email. Drop the parcel at any post office within 7 days of starting the return. Returns within the country are free; customers abroad pay the return shipping.

## Refunds
We refund to the original payment method within 5 working days of receiving the return. Delivery costs are refunded only if the whole order is returned.

## Faulty products
A product that develops a fault is handled under the warranty, not as a return. See the warranty page.
