# Delivery

## Standard delivery
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days. It costs €4.95 and is free for orders over €50.

## Express delivery
Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day can be delivered the next working day. Express delivery costs €9.95.

## Click and collect
You can collect your order free of charge from our store. Orders are ready within 2 hours during opening times and are held for 7 days.

## Restricted items
Gas canisters are classed as dangerous goods. They are sent by standard ground delivery only: they cannot be sent by express delivery or delivered abroad. An order that contains a gas canister is delivered in full by standard delivery.

## International delivery
We deliver to EU countries in 7 to 10 working days for €19.95. The destination country may charge import duties.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves the warehouse.
